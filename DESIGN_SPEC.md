## Design Concept

**One idea:** The cover does the same job as the book — it creates order out of chaos.

The Oxford Handbook of Clinical Medicine (10th ed, "Cheese & Onion") taught a generation of medics that a banded, high-contrast cover can become instantly ownable. This handbook needs the same for diplomats, soldiers, UN officials, aid workers and journalists: **distinctive, colourful, simple, durable, hopeful.**

We interpret the brief as a **three-band architecture** that will be recognised at 200 px, on a dusty dashboard, and in a stack on a diplomat's desk.

| Band | Colour | Role | Text |
|------|--------|------|------|
| **Puff band** | Warm Sand `#E9DDC4` | Top, narrow (46 px) — human, hopeful, clean | *'A must-have, must-read, manual' — Sherard Cowper-Coles* in Ink Navy, italic + caps, spaced |
| **Title band** | Ink Navy `#0F2342` | Dominant (58%) — authority, UN blue lineage, hides dirt | **THE / WAR-STOPPER'S / HANDBOOK** — all words same size, cream `#F5F1E6`, Instrument Serif, stacked |
| **Brass rules** | Brass `#B9975B` | 3 px dividers + 5 px top cap — the "ceasefire line": quiet professionalism, premium |
| **Author band** | Terracotta `#B94E33` | Lower band — warmth, humanity, hope; holds author modestly | **BARNEY MAYHEW** 12 pt, tracked 0.22 em, cream |
| **Footer** | Ink Navy + sand | Publisher logo + trim note | `PUBLISHER LOGO` on sand chip |

Background is **not white** (brief: field durability). Ink navy won't show dirt, but title stays high-contrast white-on-dark for thumbnail legibility. Subtle blind deboss grid (4% cream) suggests order-out-of-chaos without adding imagery.

---

## Typography

- **Title:** Instrument Serif / DM Serif Display, 54 pt stacked, line-height 0.86, letter-spacing -0.015 em, all caps. `THE` same size as `HANDBOOK` per 19 Oct amendment.
- **Subtitle:** Inter 11.5 pt, uppercase, 0.06 em tracking, sand-light on navy or sand.
- **Author:** Inter 12 pt, modest size, 0.22 em tracking, uppercase, cream on terracotta.
- **Puff:** Inter 10.5 pt, italic + caps mix, ink navy on sand.

All fonts web-licensed, embeddable for KDP/PDF.

## Wraparound (unfolded left→right)

`Inside Back Flap (170 px)` | `Back Cover (330 px)` | `Spine (74 px ≈37.5 mm)` | `Front Cover (330 px)` | `Inside Front Flap (170 px)`

- **Spine:** Ink navy, vertical title `THE WAR-STOPPER'S HANDBOOK` 14 pt, brass caps, author horizontal at foot, terracotta foot with spine width note.
- **Back cover:** Sand-light `#F2E8D5` for long-text readability; navy header `Praise for the Handbook`; blurb headline *What does it take to stop a war?*; 8 check bullets (strategy, genocide prevention, humanitarian, disinformation, negotiations, peacekeeping, peace deal, intl law); 3 pull quotes (Darroch, Riley, English); footer navy with price £28/$35, ISBN, barcode block.
- **Inside front flap:** Endorsements (Cowper-Coles, Darroch, Riley, English) on sand.
- **Inside back flap:** Author bio + photo placeholder on ink navy, brass accent, `www.barneymayhew.com`.

Barcode/ISBN area reserved on back footer; publisher logo slot on front bottom-right sand chip + spine.

## Colourways

Primary: **Diplomatic Navy** (presented) — best for authority + hope, works in any culture, no imagery.
Alt 1: **UN Field Green** `#1B2F26` / ochre `#B8963A` — more humanitarian field feel.
Alt 2: **Slate & Signal** `#2B2D42` / signal red `#C1121F` — harder punch for military/policy.

All use same band logic, brass stays constant for brand.
